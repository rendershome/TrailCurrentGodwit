# Build-your-own dual-CAN Pi 5 / CM5 head unit (carriers with 2+ CAN, and ways to add a second CAN bus)

Research date: 2026-11-22. About 18 tool calls. Primary sources (kernel source, vendor wikis) were used where they were reachable. Some vendor pages returned 404 or left out controller-chip details. Those gaps are listed below.

## Which CM5 carrier/IO boards have 2+ CAN onboard and a DSI connector (ideally NVMe, wide-voltage input, audio)?

### Takeaway
I found no CM5 carrier or box that is confirmed to have both 2+ onboard CAN and a DSI connector. The dual-CAN CM5 products are Seeed reComputer Industrial R21xx (2x isolated CAN FD) and EDATEC ED-IPC3630/3632 (2x isolated CAN 2.0B). Both are HDMI-only boxes with no DSI. The Waveshare CM5 bases and boxes that have DSI (IO-WIRELESS-BASE, IPCBOX-CM5, CM5-HMI-BOX-A, CM5-HMI-xA) each have only one CAN. The best "custom enclosure + DSI panel" route is a Waveshare DSI-capable CM5 base with 1 CAN, plus a second CAN controller added over SPI or USB. The alternative is a dual-CAN HDMI box driving an HDMI touch panel.

### Cited Findings
- **Seeed reComputer Industrial R21xx (CM5):** 2x isolated CAN FD ports (CAN-0, CAN-1). 2x HDMI 2.0 with no DSI listed. 1x M.2 M-key 2280 NVMe, plus a second M.2 M-key for a Hailo-8 (R21xx-12), plus an M.2 B-key for 4G/5G. DC 9-36 V input via terminal block, and PoE PD (802.3at). 4x USB-A (3x USB 3.0 + 1x USB 2.0) plus USB-C device. No audio listed, buzzer only. The wiki does not name the CAN controller chip or the device-tree overlay. — [Seeed wiki R21xx](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/recomputer_industrial_R21xx_getting_start/)
- Retailer listings name the R2136-10 (8 GB/64 GB eMMC) and R2146-10 (16 GB) variants as "CAN FD controller" models. — [OpenELAB R2136-10](https://openelab.io/products/seeed-studio-recomputer-industrial-r2136-10-raspberry-pi-cm5-can-fd-controller-8gb-ram-64gb-emmc)
- **EDATEC ED-IPC3630 / ED-IPC3632 (CM5):** 2x CAN 2.0B, 10-1000 kbps, 3 kV isolation, on a 3.5 mm terminal block. HDMI 2.0 only. CNX states that "MIPI DSI and camera interfaces from the ED-IPC3120 are not available on any ED-IPC3630 variant". M.2 Key-B 2230/2242 NVMe. 9-36 V DC input, buzzer only (no audio). 2x USB 2.0. No 40-pin header (replaced by 8 DI / 8 DO / 2 CAN). From about $300 on DigiKey (ED-IPC3632-10432-S). The CAN controller chip is not named. — [CNX Software, Mar 2025](https://www.cnx-software.com/2025/03/24/edatec-ed-ipc3630-raspberry-pi-cm5-powered-industrial-pc-di-docan-bus/)
- **EDATEC ED-IPC3120:** has a 30-pin 0.5 mm FPC 4-lane DSI connector. CAN on this model was not confirmed in search results. — [CNX ED-IPC3100 series](https://www.cnx-software.com/2024/12/11/EDATEC-ED-IPC3100-RASPBERRY-PI-CM5-BASED-INDUSTRIAL-COMPUTERS/), [EDATEC ED-IPC3100 datasheet (Mouser)](https://www.mouser.lt/datasheet/2/1563/EDATEC_01_16_2025_ED_IPC3100_datasheet_en-3541015.pdf)
- **Waveshare CM5 bases and boxes with DSI but one CAN:** CM5-IO-WIRELESS-BASE (1x isolated CAN up to 1 Mbps, dual MIPI DSI/CSI). IPCBOX-CM5-A/B (one CAN, dual MIPI, 120R jumper). CM5-HMI-BOX-A (one CAN, dual MIPI). CM5-DUAL-ETH-BASE and CM5-DUAL-ETH-4G/5G-BASE (dual MIPI, no CAN found). — [docs.waveshare.com CM5-IO-WIRELESS-BASE](https://docs.waveshare.com/CM5-IO-WIRELESS-BASE), [IPCBOX-CM5-A](https://docs.waveshare.com/IPCBOX-CM5-A), [CM5-HMI-BOX-A](https://docs.waveshare.com/CM5-HMI-BOX-A), [CNX dual-Ethernet CM5 carrier, 7-36 V](https://www.cnx-software.com/2025/08/21/raspberry-pi-cm5-carrier-board-supports-dual-ethernet-1gbe-2-5gbe-and-wide-7v-36v-dc-input/)
- **Sfera Labs Strato Pi Max (CM5, DIN rail):** modular, with up to four internal X2 expansion slots supporting "RS232/485, CAN, digital & analog I/O and UPS". There is an X2 "CAN and Dual RS-485" expansion board. Two CAN ports would presumably need two X2 CAN boards (my inference). It has no display connector. — [CNX Strato Pi Max](https://www.cnx-software.com/2024/12/12/sfera-labs-strato-pi-max-din-rail-industrial-controllers-raspberry-pi-compute-module-5/?amp=1&noamp=mobile), [X2 CAN board user guide (Mouser)](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/866/X2BS10XC44_user_guide-3460278.pdf)
- **Sfera Labs Strato Pi Plus (Pi 4B/Pi 5 based, not CM5):** quad RS-485 and CAN FD (July 2026). — [LinuxGizmos](https://linuxgizmos.com/sfera-labs-ships-strato-pi-plus-with-quad-rs-485-and-can-fd/)
- **Official Raspberry Pi CM5IO board:** no CAN in the datasheet. — [cm5io datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/cm5/cm5io-datasheet.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a DSI touch panel in a custom enclosure, the most practical CM5 path is a Waveshare CM5-IO-WIRELESS-BASE or IPCBOX-CM5 (DSI + 1 isolated CAN + wide-voltage input on several Waveshare CM5 bases), plus one extra CAN from the options in the next section.
- Seeed R21xx is the strongest true-dual-CAN CM5 option: CAN FD, isolated, 9-36 V, NVMe, 4x USB-A. It would need an HDMI touch display (touch over USB costs a USB port) instead of DSI, and an external sound card (another USB port). On a USB-constrained build that is a real cost.
- EDATEC ED-IPC3630 has only 2x USB 2.0 and an M.2 B-key 2242 slot, which makes it a worse fit than Seeed for this USB-heavy build.
- The CAN controllers on Seeed and EDATEC are not named in the sources. They are most likely SPI MCP251x parts (the Pi has no native CAN), but this is unverified. That matters for confirming mainline driver support.

### Gaps
- Mcuzone, Gumstix, Kunbus (RevPi Connect 5 / RevPi CON CAN), Hardkernel-style carriers, and open-hardware KiCad CM5 carriers with dual CAN were not covered within the tool budget. A GitHub search for "CM5 carrier KiCad CAN" is still needed.
- CAN controller chip and overlay names for the Seeed R21xx and EDATEC ED-IPC3630 were not found in vendor pages fetched this session.
- Whether the ED-IPC3120 (the DSI model) has any CAN at all is unconfirmed.
- Pricing for Seeed R21xx was not stated on the wiki.

## Second-CAN options for a single-CAN panel or a Pi 5, ranked, with driver/mainline/firmware/FD/isolation/price/port cost

### Takeaway
In order of fit: (1) an SPI-attached MCP2518FD/MCP2515 on a free SPI bus, which uses the mainline `mcp251xfd`/`mcp251x` drivers and costs no USB port. This is already proven in Godwit on the Pi 5 via the Waveshare 2-CH CAN FD HAT. It is only possible if the panel exposes SPI pins. (2) An M.2/mini-PCIe CAN card if an M.2 slot is free. The PEAK PCAN-M.2 is 1/2/4-channel, isolated and CAN FD, but its mainline driver coverage needs confirming. On the CM5-HMI that slot is probably the NVMe slot, so this conflicts with NVMe. (3) A dual-channel USB adapter with an in-kernel driver (`peak_usb` PCAN-USB Pro FD, `kvaser_usb` 2xHS models, `f81604`, `gs_usb` multi-channel), so one USB port gives both buses. A single-channel candleLight/gs_usb device is the cheapest fully open-firmware option, but it costs one USB port per bus.

### Cited Findings

**(1) Internal/SPI options**
- **Waveshare CM5-HMI-xA (7A/8A/10.1A):** one isolated CAN, up to 1 Mbps. The page does not state the controller chip, the SPI attachment, or the dtoverlay. GPIOs are referenced individually (GPIO2/3, 7, 11, 16, 22-27, buzzer on GPIO7, ATSHA204/EEPROM on I2C1). A full 40-pin header is not confirmed. 2x USB 2.0 + 2x USB 3.2 Gen1 + USB-C. M.2 M-key (NVMe/AI) + M.2 B-key (4G/5G) + nano SIM. 7-36 V DC. No audio, buzzer only. — [docs.waveshare.com CM5-HMI-7A-ACCE](https://docs.waveshare.com/CM5-HMI-7A-ACCE)
- Waveshare marketing for the CM5-HMI-xA-ACCE DSI version says it leaves the "Raspberry Pi 40PIN GPIO free". This suggests that a 40-pin header (and so SPI0/SPI1 pins) may be accessible, but it is not confirmed in the docs. — [Waveshare CM5-HMI-xA-ACCE product page](https://www.waveshare.com/cm5-hmi-xa-acce.htm)
- **Waveshare 2-CH CAN FD HAT:** 2x MCP2518FD, isolated up to 5 kV. Mode A uses two SPI buses (SPI0-0 IRQ 25, SPI1-0 IRQ 24). Mode B uses one shared SPI0 (CE0/CE1). The mainline overlay for mode A is `dtoverlay=spi1-3cs`, `dtoverlay=mcp251xfd,spi0-0,interrupt=25`, `dtoverlay=mcp251xfd,spi1-0,interrupt=24` (kernel >= 5.4.77 per Waveshare). The legacy overlay is `waveshare-can-fd-hat-mode-a`. The wiki lists Pi Zero through 4B and does not mention the Pi 5. — [Waveshare wiki 2-CH CAN FD HAT](https://waveshare.com/wiki/2-CH_CAN_FD_HAT)
- Waveshare's other SPI CAN boards use the MCP2515 over SPI (e.g. RS485 CAN for Jetson Nano). — [Waveshare wiki](https://waveshare.com/wiki/RS485_CAN_for_Jetson_Nano)

**(2) M.2 / mini-PCIe cards**
- **PEAK PCAN-M.2:** single, dual or four-channel (IPEH-004083/-004084/-004085). CAN 2.0A/B and CAN FD (up to 12 Mbit/s data phase). Galvanic isolation up to 300 V, per channel. Form factor 2280/2260 B-M key. Card height 4.6 mm for 1-2 channels, 10.2 mm for 4 channels. Uses one PCIe lane. No public price. The single-channel version was out of stock at fetch time. Linux driver support is via PEAK's Linux driver page, and the product page does not mention the mainline driver. — [PEAK PCAN-M.2](https://www.peak-system.com/PCAN-M-2.473.0.html?&L=1)
- The mainline Kconfig `CAN_PEAK_PCIEFD` help text names only "PEAK-System PCI Express FD CAN-FD cards family" / PCAN-PCI Express FD. It does not mention PCAN-M.2 or miniPCIe FD. — [drivers/net/can/peak_canfd/Kconfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/peak_canfd/Kconfig)
- The fetched `drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig` summary lists "Kvaser Mini PCI Express 2xHS" among Kvaser devices. That entry is a PCIe device and is probably handled by `kvaser_pciefd`, not `kvaser_usb`. The summarizer may have conflated them, so verify this. — [drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig)

**(3) USB-CAN adapters with in-kernel drivers**
- **gs_usb (Geschwister Schneider / candleLight protocol):** the mainline ID table includes Geschwister Schneider USB/CAN 1d50:606f, candleLight 1209:2323, CES CANext FD 1cd2:606f, ABE CANdebugger FD 16d0:10b8, Xylanta SAINT3 16d0:0f30, and CANnectivity 1209:ca01. The channel count comes from the device-reported `icount` (u8, so multi-channel is supported by the driver). CAN FD is supported via the `GS_CAN_FEATURE_FD` and `GS_CAN_FEATURE_BT_CONST_EXT` feature flags. — [drivers/net/can/usb/gs_usb.c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/usb/gs_usb.c)
- **candleLight FD (Linux Automation):** STM32G0B1, CAN FD capable. Ships with one channel, with footprints for a second channel present but unpopulated. Firmware is based on candleLight_fw (`BOARD_candleLightFD`), with changes being upstreamed. Hardware is licensed CERN OHL v1.2. Isolation is not mentioned. Sold as an assembled, tested kit. — [linux-automation/candleLightFD README](https://github.com/linux-automation/candleLightFD/blob/main/README.md)
- **peak_usb (mainline):** PCAN-USB, PCAN-USB FD, **PCAN-USB Pro (2ch CAN 2.0)**, **PCAN-USB Pro FD (2ch CAN FD)**, PCAN-Chip USB, PCAN-USB X6 (6ch FD). — [drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig)
- **kvaser_usb (mainline):** many dual-channel models, including USBcan Pro 2xHS v2, Memorator Pro 2xHS v2, USBcan Light 2xHS, USBcan II HS/HS, Hybrid 2xCAN/LIN, plus 4xHS/5xHS models and single-channel Leaf Light v2/Leaf v3/U100. — [drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig)
- **Other mainline USB-CAN drivers:** `f81604` (Fintek F81604 USB-to-2CAN, CAN 2.0A/B, with terminator-control pins), `etas_es58x` (ES581.4, ES582.1, ES584.1), `esd_usb` (CAN-USB/2, CAN-USB/3-FD, CAN-USB/Micro), `ucan` (Theobroma Seal), `usb_8dev` (USB2CAN), `mcba_usb` (Microchip CAN BUS Analyzer). — [drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/net/can/usb/Kconfig)
- **Waveshare USB-CAN-FD (dual-channel, isolated):** the base model is Windows-only ("does not support Linux"). The USB-CAN-FD-B variant adds Linux support, but the search results do not say whether it uses gs_usb or a vendor driver. — [Waveshare wiki USB-CAN-FD](https://waveshare.com/wiki/USB-CAN-FD), [Waveshare USB-CAN-FD product](https://www.waveshare.com/usb-can-fd.htm)
- **Waveshare USB-CAN-B:** dual-channel, industrial isolation. The Linux driver type is not established. — [Waveshare USB-CAN-B](https://www.waveshare.com/product/iot-communication/wired-comm-converter/usb-can-b.htm)
- **HMS Ixxat USB-to-CAN FD Pro (2ch, isolated) and ICP DAS I-7565M-FD (2ch):** neither was shown to have a mainline SocketCAN driver. The I-7565M-FD lists Windows drivers. — [HMS](https://www.hms-networks.com/p/1-01-0383-22002-usb-to-can-fd-pro), [ICP DAS](https://www.icpdas.com/en/product/I-7565M-FD)

### Inferences
- **Ranking for Godwit** (2x classic CAN at 500 kbit/s, SocketCAN, fully open source, USB-scarce):
  1. **SPI MCP2518FD (`mcp251xfd`) or MCP2515 (`mcp251x`) on a free SPI bus/CS + GPIO IRQ.** Costs no USB port, is all mainline, and matches the existing Pi 5 HAT configuration. On the CM5-HMI-xA this works only if the 40-pin header (or SPI pins) is physically reachable and the onboard CAN does not already use the same SPI/CS/IRQ. This must be checked against the CM5-HMI schematic or its CAN usage guide. On a bare Pi 5, the existing Waveshare 2-CH CAN FD HAT already gives both buses. Note that the HAT wiki does not list the Pi 5, but Godwit already uses it there.
  2. **M.2 CAN (PEAK PCAN-M.2, B-M key, isolated, 1/2/4 channels).** Costs no USB port, but takes the M.2 slot. On the CM5-HMI the M-key slot is normally the NVMe slot, so this trades storage, unless the B-key 4G/5G slot can carry PCIe (unverified). Mainline support for PCAN-M.2 under `peak_pciefd` is not confirmed by Kconfig text and must be checked against the PCI ID table in `peak_pciefd_main.c`.
  3. **One dual-channel USB adapter with a mainline driver**, so one USB port gives two buses: PEAK PCAN-USB Pro FD (`peak_usb`, isolated per PEAK's usual spec; isolation and price not verified here), Kvaser USBcan Pro 2xHS v2 (`kvaser_usb`), or a Fintek F81604-based 2CAN dongle (`f81604`, classic CAN only, cheapest). These drivers are mainline, but device firmware is proprietary on PEAK and Kvaser. The "fully open source" requirement concerns drivers, and those are GPL in-kernel.
  4. **Single-channel gs_usb/candleLight devices** (CANable 2.x, candleLight FD). These have fully open firmware (candleLight_fw) and open hardware (CERN OHL for candleLight FD), but cost one USB port per bus. A candleLight FD with the second-channel footprint populated could in principle give 2 channels on one port, but no shipping 2-channel build was confirmed.
- For a single-CAN panel needing only one extra bus, any option above works. The SPI route is the only one that keeps all four USB-A ports free.
- At 500 kbit/s classic CAN, CAN FD capability is optional. MCP2515/F81604 class parts are sufficient.

### Gaps
- Whether the CM5-HMI-10.1A exposes a full 40-pin header with SPI pins, and which SPI/CS/IRQ its onboard CAN uses. The `docs.waveshare.com/CM5-HMI-10.1A` and `waveshare.com/wiki/CM5-HMI-10.1A` URLs returned 404. The linked "CAN Usage Guide" was not fetched.
- Chipsee PPC-CM5-101 internal headers and SPI exposure were not researched in this session.
- Mainline support for PEAK PCAN-M.2 / PCAN-miniPCIe FD under `peak_pciefd` (PCI ID table) and for Kvaser M.2/mini-PCIe under `kvaser_pciefd` was not verified against the source.
- CANable 2.x specifics (MCU, gs_usb firmware support versus slcan-only stock firmware), isolation, and prices for CANable, PCAN-USB Pro FD, Kvaser 2xHS and F81604 dongles were not retrieved.
- The Linux driver type for Waveshare USB-CAN-FD-B and USB-CAN-B (gs_usb versus a vendor out-of-tree driver) is unknown.
- `mcp251xfd` and `mcp251x` are in mainline under `drivers/net/can/spi/` (`mcp251xfd` since roughly v5.10, from background knowledge). This was not re-verified against kernel.org in this session.
